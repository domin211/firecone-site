Privacy Policy

Firecone values privacy and transparency. This policy outlines the data we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you—including GDPR rights for users in the European Union.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Firecone ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our hosting services, websites, or communication channels. Firecone is established in the Czech Republic and follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable laws.

2. Data we collect

Account information: email address, username, timezone, and optional profile details used to provision servers and communicate with you.

Service data: server configurations and panel usage metrics collected to operate, secure, improve, and troubleshoot the Service.

Communications: support tickets, emails, and moderation reports recorded to manage abuse investigations and respond to requests.

3. How we use data

We use personal data to operate and maintain the Service, including provisioning servers, processing backups, and delivering notifications about incidents or updates; to protect the platform from abuse by running security checks, verifying compliance with our Terms & Conditions, and investigating policy violations; and to improve the Service through aggregated analytics, feedback, and volunteer training.

4. Cookies & tracking

This marketing website is a static site and does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or a cookie-consent banner.

When you sign in to the Firecone panel or client area, we use first-party cookies or similar technologies to maintain your login session. Rejecting those cookies may limit account functionality, but this public website will still work.

5. Legal bases for processing

We process personal data under the following legal bases: (a) performance of a contract when delivering the hosting Service, (b) legitimate interest when preventing abuse or improving the platform, and (c) consent where it is required for a specific optional use.

6. Sharing & transfers

We do not sell personal data to unaffiliated entities. We share information only with trusted vendors that provide infrastructure, security, or other services needed to run the platform.

If data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we pick providers that fulfill the law of the European Union.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to deliver the Service, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Inactive servers may be archived or deleted after advance notice. Backups are cycled on a rolling schedule and purged when they are no longer required.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to request access to your personal data, correct inaccuracies, delete information, restrict processing, receive a portable copy, and object to certain uses. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise your rights, email firecone.eu@gmail.com. We will respond within one month unless a longer period is permitted by law.

9. Data security

We implement technical and organizational measures such as encrypted transport, access controls, logging, and staff training. Despite these safeguards, no internet transmission is completely secure, so we encourage you to maintain your own backups and report suspected incidents promptly.

10. Children's privacy

Firecone is not directed to children under 13. We request that minors obtain parental or guardian consent before creating an account or using the Service. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child without consent, we will delete it quickly.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. When changes are significant we will notify all emails that do not correspond to a deleted account in our database.

12. Contact us

For questions or complaints regarding privacy, email firecone.eu@gmail.com, or contact us on Discord or the contact page.
